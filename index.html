About Us

A health-tech venture for safer, independent living

Who We Are

Safe Watch App is building a health and emergency monitoring solution for elderly people, chronic-care users and individuals who live alone.

Our goal is to help families and caregivers receive immediate updates when something goes wrong, without requiring the user to operate a complex device or smartphone.

The product is already beyond the idea stage: a functional MVP has been developed, core features have been tested, and the next focus is hardware optimisation, battery autonomy and commercial readiness.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make independent living safer by giving families and caregivers a simple way to know when their loved one may need help.

Safe Watch App combines automatic fall detection, health measurements, GPS location and emergency alerts into one caregiver-first smartwatch solution.

What Drives Us

Europe is ageing, families are under pressure, and many elderly people spend hours without direct supervision. In emergencies such as falls, fainting, abnormal heart rate or low oxygen levels, delayed response can have serious consequences.

We are building Safe Watch App to reduce that delay and give families peace of mind through automatic monitoring and immediate alerts.

How It Works

Simple setup, powerful protection

1

Install & Setup

Download the app to your smartwatch and complete a quick setup process. Add your emergency contacts and customize your preferences.

2

Continuous Monitoring

The app runs in the background, continuously measuring your heart rate, blood pressure, and blood oxygen levels throughout the day.

3

Intelligent Analysis

Our monitoring algorithms analyze your vitals in real-time, learning your baseline and detecting any concerning deviations.

4

Automatic Alerts

If a fall is detected or vitals fall outside safe ranges, the app immediately sends notifications to your emergency contacts with your location and status.

5

Manual Updates

You can also manually update your status at any time using a button or voice command to let your contacts know you're fine or need assistance.
